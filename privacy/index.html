Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

431 Marketing LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates mysharpestfriend.com and provides sales and service as an independent Cutco sales representative. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, including your name, phone number, email address, mailing address, and details about your purchases or service requests. We collect it when you fill out our sign-up form online or in person, or when you contact us.

How we use your information

  • Schedule and confirm sharpening appointments and visits
  • Follow up on orders and answer product questions
  • Send product care tips, promotions, and event invitations
  • Respond to your messages and requests

Text messaging

If you opt in to receive text messages, we use your mobile number to send the messages described in our SMS Terms. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

No sharing of mobile information

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or text message opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as our text messaging platform) that process it only to deliver our messages.

Other sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share order details with Cutco Corporation only as needed to process orders, warranties, or service you request; Cutco's handling of that information is covered by Cutco's Privacy Policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, at any time using the contact details below.
