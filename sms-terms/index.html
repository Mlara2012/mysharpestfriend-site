Program description

431 Marketing LLC (Matthew Lara, independent Cutco sales representative) sends text messages about sharpening appointments and reminders, order follow-ups, product care tips, promotions, and customer event invitations.

How you opt in

You opt in by entering your mobile number and agreeing to text messages on our sign-up form, either online through mysharpestfriend.com or in person when you meet with us. The form includes the consent statement shown on our homepage. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your wireless carrier for details.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. To rejoin, reply START or sign up again.

Help

Privacy
